Talkaside listens to a live call on your PC, transcribes both sides as they speak, and gives you two things in real time: a suggested answer when someone asks you a question, and a translation when the other person speaks a language you don't. It works with whatever call app you already use, because it captures the audio from Windows itself.

## What it does

**Answer suggestions during interviews.** Two AI assistants read the live transcript together with your CV, the job description and your own Q&A bank, and draft an answer you can say in your own words. You choose the providers and hold the keys.

**Two-way live translation.** Each side of the call is translated as it is spoken, in both directions, so you can follow and reply in the language you are comfortable with.

**My Voice.** Record 10 to 60 seconds of your voice and Talkaside builds a local voice profile that is used only for your outgoing translated speech. This feature needs an NVIDIA GPU.

**Local history.** Transcripts and sessions are stored on your own machine under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Talkaside`. Recording the call is optional and off until you turn it on.

**Float panel.** A small always-on-top window keeps suggestions next to your call. It is built to stay out of supported screen captures so your own notes are not shown when you share your screen. Capture tools differ, so check it with yours before an important call. It does not make the use of an assistant undetectable and is not meant to.

## Privacy

Talkaside has no server of its own in the audio path. Speech and text go to the speech and AI providers you configure, and nowhere else. Those providers see what you send them, so choose them accordingly.

## Requirements and price

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. There is no macOS, Linux or mobile version.
- A 5-day demo is free, with one activation per Windows device.
- Paid licences are available for 7 days, 30 days or a year.
- Cloud AI and speech recognition are billed separately by the providers you connect.
- The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning on first run.
