AI interview assistant that suggests answers in real time

Talkaside listens to the interviewer, writes a live transcript and drafts an answer you can say in your own words.

Try the 5-day demo
  • Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit
  • 5 days, free, one activation per device
  • No Talkaside server in the path

Job interviews move fast. Talkaside runs on your Windows PC next to the call app you already use. When the interviewer asks a question, two AI assistants read the live transcript together with your CV, the job description and your own Q&A bank, and draft a short answer. You stay in the conversation and decide what to say.

How the interview assistant works

  1. Add your CV and the job

    Paste your CV and the job description so answers match your experience and the role.

  2. Join the call

    Open Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet as usual and press Start in Talkaside.

  3. Read the suggestion

    Each question is transcribed as it is asked, and a suggested answer appears on your screen.

What helps during the interview

Two AI assistants

Choose the AI providers you trust. Two assistants can answer the same question in different ways.

Your Q&A bank

Add answers you prepared in advance, and Talkaside uses them when a matching question comes up.

Float panel

Keep suggestions in a small always-on-top window, built to stay out of supported screen shares.

Review afterwards

Every interview is saved on your PC with its transcript and the AI answers, so you can prepare for the next round.

Interview assistant questions

Does it work with Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet?

Yes. Talkaside takes the call audio from Windows, so it works with the call app you already use.

Which AI does it use?

You choose the AI providers and connect them in the settings. Two assistants can run side by side.

Does it know my experience?

Yes, if you add your CV and the job description. Answers are based on them and on your Q&A bank.

Is there a free version?

There is a free 5 day demo with one activation per device. Paid licences are available for 7 days, 30 days or a year.

What does it need to run?

Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit, and an internet connection for the cloud AI and speech providers you choose.
