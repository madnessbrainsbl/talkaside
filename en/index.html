Questions, answered

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11, 64 bit. There is no macOS, Linux or mobile version.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. It lasts 5 days with one activation per device. Cloud AI and speech providers bill you separately.

Where does my audio go?

Only to the speech and AI providers you configure. No Talkaside server sits in the audio path.

Why does Windows show a warning?

The installer is not code signed, so SmartScreen may warn on first run.

Can I keep my calls?

Transcripts and sessions are stored on your PC. Recording is optional and off until you turn it on.
